# 🐙 The Adventures of Octoking

**The Adventures of Octoking (AOC)** is a multiplayer **1v1 turn-based battle game** built with [p5.js](https://p5js.org/).

Choose your companion, build your strategy, and battle your opponent using attacks, defenses, healing, and powerful special abilities. As you play, you can unlock new characters and expand your collection.

> **⚔️ Battle. 🧪 Strategize. 🎴 Collect. 👑 Become the Octoking.**

---

## 🎮 Play AOC

AOC can be played directly in your browser:

**[▶️ Play The Adventures of Octoking](https://tempest2121.github.io/The-Adventures-of-Octoking/)**

For the best experience:

* Use **landscape orientation**
* A resolution of approximately **1400 × 900 or higher** is recommended
* If your screen is smaller, adjust your browser's **zoom level** until the game fits comfortably

No installation is required. Open the game, choose your character, and start battling!

## ⚔️ How to Play

AOC is a **turn-based 1v1 battle game** centered around four core actions:

| Action               | Description                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ⚔️ **Attack**        | Deals damage to your opponent.                                                                |
| 🛡️ **Defense**      | Has a chance to completely negate an incoming attack; otherwise, it reduces the damage taken. |
| ❤️ **Heal**          | Restores a certain amount of health.                                                          |
| ✨ **Special Attack** | Uses your character's unique ability once enough mana has been accumulated.                   |

Each character has its own combination of **strengths, weaknesses, buffs, and debuffs**, meaning that choosing the right action at the right time is essential.

### 🧠 Strategy Matters

AOC isn't simply about dealing the most damage possible.

Knowing **when to attack, defend, heal, or save your mana for a special attack** can determine the outcome of a battle.

## 🐙 Characters & Companions

AOC currently features **8 playable characters**, each with their own abilities, buffs, and debuffs.

Every player begins with two basic companions:

* 🐱 **Octokat**
* 🐙 **Octopus**

Other companions can be unlocked as **collectible characters** through the in-game gacha system.

Each new character brings a different playstyle to the battlefield, allowing players to experiment with different strategies and combinations.

### 🎴 Collect Them All

Characters aren't just alternate skins — they are unique battle companions with their own gameplay characteristics.

After battles, players can work toward expanding their collection and discovering new companions.
